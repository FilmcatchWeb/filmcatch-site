One-liner

Catch movies in the real world — a location-based game where films spawn around you to collect, complete, and trade.

Description

FilmCatch is a location-based iOS game for discovering and collecting movies. Films spawn at real places on a map of the player's neighborhood — scan to reveal what's nearby, walk into range, and catch them. Every catch becomes a collectible card with the film's story, cast, and where it's streaming right now, turning each find into a "watch it tonight" moment.

Rarity is earned from real-world acclaim, movie theaters are in-game hotspots, new releases spawn as limited PREMIERE editions, and after dark the horror films come out. Players curate showcases of their favorites, chase holographic Shinies, complete themed sets, and trade with collectors worldwide using in-game currency. FilmCatch is free to play and built to celebrate film — every card links out to where the movie can legally be watched.
